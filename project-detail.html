PROJECT OVERVIEW & SPECIFICATIONS

Sub-grade engineered pathways safeguarding historical topology.

Execution of critical medium-voltage (MV) culvert infrastructure and reinforced underground service corridors supporting the Diriyah Gate mega-development in Riyadh. Elevate Builders mobilized specialized civil crews to deliver precision cast-in-place concrete box culverts, deep sub-grade trenching, and utility duct banks under rigorous geotechnical constraints.

The scope demanded uncompromising adherence to Diriyah Gate Development Authority (DGDA) heritage buffer engineering protocols and expedited handover schedules. Structural waterproofing, heavy backfilling, and surface reinstatement were completed in strict conformance with Saudi Building Code standards.
