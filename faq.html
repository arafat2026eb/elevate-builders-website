Key operational, regulatory, and technical information regarding Elevate Builders: corporate registration, ISO governance, Ajeer compliance, execution capabilities, and tendering procedures in Saudi Arabia.

Elevate Builders is a fully licensed general contracting entity legally registered with the Saudi Ministry of Commerce under Commercial Registration (CR) Number 7049953909.

We maintain active tax clearance with ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority), registration with the Riyadh Chamber of Commerce, and active vendor pre-qualifications with leading national developers, industrial authorities, and utility providers.

Elevate Builders operates under an Integrated Management System governed by three internationally accredited ISO certifications:

  • ISO 9001:2015: Quality Management System ensuring systematic inspection, testing plans (ITP), and material verification.
  • ISO 14001:2015: Environmental Management System governing sustainable site waste disposal, dust suppression, and spill prevention.
  • ISO 45001:2018: Occupational Health and Safety Management System mandating daily pre-shift toolbox briefings, PPE enforcement, and incident prevention.

Yes. Elevate Builders is an official certified applicator for multinational building chemical manufacturers, including Fosroc and DCP (Don Construction Products).

Our specialized teams are certified for high-tolerance industrial epoxy flooring, polyurea protective linings, chemical-resistant bund wall coatings, elastomeric joint sealants, and structural concrete repair systems.

We deliver comprehensive execution across five defined sectors:

  • 01 Civil & Structural Works: Deep raft foundations, monolithic concrete casting, structural steel framing, and industrial retention bunkers.
  • 02 Infrastructure Works: Sub-grade dry/wet utilities, medium-voltage (MV) culverts, stormwater conduits, and high-density bulk earthworks.
  • 03 Architectural Fit-out & Finishing: Turnkey commercial interiors, acoustic wall systems, porcelain tiling, and calibrated epoxy floors.
  • 04 Manpower Supply: Certified trade crews (carpenters, steel fixers, technicians) deployed with 100% Ajeer contractual compliance.
  • 05 Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing (MEP): Industrial HVAC ducting, power substations, fire protection piping, and sanitary drainage.

All engineering design coordination, material selection, and field execution comply strictly with the Saudi Building Code (SBC):

Structural works adhere to SBC-301 and SBC-801; electrical systems to SBC-401 and IEEE standards; mechanical installations to SBC-501 and ASHRAE 90.1; and fire life safety systems to NFPA 13, NFPA 72, and Saudi Civil Defense regulations. Material quality is independently verified via ASTM compression, slump, and density testing.

Yes, 100% of our mobilized trade personnel and field staff operate under legal, verifiable contractual agreements processed directly through the Ministry of Human Resources' Ajeer and Qiwa platforms.

We issue electronic Ajeer notices prior to site entry, provide full medical insurance, maintain centralized company camp lodging, and guarantee compliance with Saudi labour legislation.

For projects situated within Riyadh and the Central Province, initial crew mobilization begins within 48 to 72 hours following contract signing, Ajeer electronic contract activation, and security gate pass issuance.

For large-scale remote infrastructure programs or major regional developments (e.g., Western Region or Eastern Province), phased workforce mobilization is scheduled according to approved project baseline programs.

Elevate Builders is headquartered in Riyadh, with primary mobilization concentration across the Riyadh Province (including Diriyah Gate, King Salman Park corridors, and industrial logistics parks).

We also maintain operational logistics to mobilize execution teams and equipment across the Eastern Province (Dammam, Khobar, Jubail), Western Region (Jeddah, Makkah), and strategic giga-project developments Kingdom-wide.

We offer flexible contracting models tailored to project scale:

  • Turnkey Contracting: Complete design coordination, procurement, civil structure, MEP, and architectural finishing for commercial and industrial facilities.
  • Specialized Trade Sub-contracting: Discrete packages for main contractors, such as heavy foundation casting, subterranean MV culverts, certified Fosroc/DCP epoxy flooring, or MEP installations.
